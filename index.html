Seriously, what's the catch?

There isn't one. I spend a few hours building your preview because I'm betting you'll like it. If you don't, you owe nothing and we part as friends. The preview stays private and is never shown to a competitor.

What's in the free preview?

A real, working one-page site: your name, your services, hours, map, tap-to-call, and your actual Google reviews quoted on the page. It's watermarked and sits on a private link until you say go.

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and every file is yours. If you're on Hosting & Care, you can cancel after 12 months and I hand the files over. No hostage-taking.

Text me. On Hosting & Care, small changes (prices, hours, staff, photos) are unlimited and done within a day. On Hosting Lite they're $25 each. Self-hosters get a plain-English guide and 30 days of email support.
